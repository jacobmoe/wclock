Privacy Policy

WClock Bedroom Clock · Last updated October 6, 2026

WClock Bedroom Clock does not collect, store, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Location

The app asks for your approximate location while you are using it. It uses this only to show the weather for where you are and to work out local sunrise and sunset for the automatic day/night switch. Your location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit weather service to get that weather. The developer does not receive or keep it. The app saves your last location on your device only, so the weather can load faster next time. You can turn location access off any time in Settings, but then no weather will be shown.

Data on your device

Your settings (day/night choice, temperature unit, dimming) and the most recent weather are stored on your device only.

Screen brightness

If dimming is turned on, the app lowers the screen brightness at night and restores your brightness afterwards. It does not read or send any information about it.

Third parties

Weather data is provided by Apple WeatherKit. Apple's own privacy policy applies to its service: apple.com/legal/privacy.
