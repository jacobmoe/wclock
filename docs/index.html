WClock Bedroom Clock

A simple, dim bedside clock for iPad. It shows the time, the date and the current weather, keeps the screen awake while it's open, and switches between a day and a night look at sunrise and sunset.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email yakofnyc@gmail.com.

Tips

  • Tap the … in the top-right corner for settings: day/night, °F/°C, and screen dimming.
  • At night, tap the screen to brighten it for 15 seconds.
  • The weather needs location access (approximate is fine) and an internet connection.
  • For an all-night clock, keep the iPad plugged in.
